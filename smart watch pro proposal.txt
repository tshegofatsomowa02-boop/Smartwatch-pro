Smart Watch Pro Website Proposal

Introduction

The purpose of this project is to create a website that advertises and promotes the Smart Watch Pro. The website will give customers information about the product and show them why they should consider buying it.

Aim of the Website
The main aim is to create a simple and attractive website where customers can learn about the Smart Watch Pro.The website will include pictures, information about the product, its features and the price.

Website Features

* A home page
* Pictures of the Smart Watch Pro
* Product information
* A list of features
* The price of the product
* An “Add to Cart” or “Buy Now” button
* A contact section
* Navigation buttons

Target Audience
* Students
* Young people
* People who like technology
* People who exercise
* People looking for a smartwatch

Advertisement:
The website will advertise the Smart Watch Pro by using pictures, short descriptions and information about its features. The website will have a simple design that makes the product easy to see.
